COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Carry Forward:
Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused leave days to the next calendar year.

Leave Application:
Employees must submit leave requests through the employee portal.

Manager Approval:
Leave requests longer than 5 consecutive working days require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

Emergency Leave:
Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when taking emergency leave.

Work From Home:
Employees may request work-from-home arrangements subject to manager approval and company policy.
